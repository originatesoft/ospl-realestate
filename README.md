## What Is OSPL Real Estate

OSPL Real Estate is a complete **[real estate CRM software](https://originatesoft.com/real-estate-crm-software/)** developed by **Originate Soft Pvt Ltd**, a custom software and web development company based in Kolkata, India.

Property sales run on timing. An enquiry that waits a day for a reply is usually a lead lost to a competitor. Most agencies lose deals not because of the market, but because leads sit in WhatsApp chats, listings live in spreadsheets, and follow-ups live in someone's memory. OSPL Real Estate replaces that scatter with one system built around how property businesses actually work.

The name is the promise:

- **Manage** — leads, properties, clients, agents, and tasks, all in one dashboard.
- **Sell** — move every enquiry through a clear pipeline from first call to closed deal.
- **Grow** — use real numbers on sources, agent performance, and conversions to decide where to put your effort.

**Built for:**

- Real estate agencies and brokerages
- Property dealers and independent agents
- Builders and developers managing project inventory and site visits
- Rental and property management businesses
- Teams handling residential, commercial, or plot inventory

## Why a Dedicated Real Estate CRM

A general-purpose CRM treats every contact the same. A real estate business does not. You are matching a person's budget and preferences to a specific property, tracking site visits, and juggling several deals per client. OSPL Real Estate is structured around that: contacts, properties, visits, and deals are linked, so you see the full picture instead of four separate lists.

## How to Get Started

**SaaS (Annual Subscription)**

1. **Share your requirements** — team size, property types, and lead sources.
2. **Choose your plan** — Originate Soft confirms the annual subscription that fits your team.
3. **Account setup** — your pipeline stages, roles, and fields are configured.
4. **Team onboarding** — a walkthrough so your agents can start using the CRM right away.

**Self-Hosted / Custom**

1. **Share your requirements** — workflows, integrations, and customization needs.
2. **Scope and proposal** — the team reviews your requirements and shares scope, timeline, and pricing.
3. **Environment setup** — server, domain, SSL, and database are prepared on your infrastructure.
4. **Custom development and configuration** — modules, pipeline, roles, and branding are built to your workflow.
5. **Testing and handover** — end-to-end testing, team walkthrough, and documentation.
6. **Go live** — your team starts working in the CRM, with ongoing support for fixes, updates, and new features.
